## 1. Purpose

The public About page for the Love in Action Database. It opens with the "Join Us in Changing the World" navy band moved off PB-00, then tells the origin story of the program, explains that it is free for every Alliance member organization, lists what the program has actually delivered, and offers the two primary calls to action. It closes with a navy band carrying Heidi White's TEDx talk as an embedded video.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Arrives from:** the ABOUT link in the global navigation (MP-02), the About link in the public footer, and direct links.

**Leaves to:**
- `/items` — public item browse (Provide an Item button, client-side)
- `/volunteer` — public volunteer browse (Volunteer Your Time button, client-side)
- YouTube — the embedded TEDx video plays in place; the viewer may follow it out to youtube.com through the player's own controls.

## 3. Data

**Reads:** Nothing.
**Writes:** Nothing.
**Functions called:** None.
**Never touches:** anything. This surface is content only.

## 4. Layout regions

Top to bottom:

1. **Top navy band** — "Join Us in Changing the World" heading + the "Each of us have talents…" paragraph. Reuses `.pb0-navy` / `.pb0-navy-heading` / `.pb0-navy-para`, unchanged from how it read on PB-00. This is the page headline, not one section among several.
2. **Origin story** — `.pb6-section`, "When a Kid Needs a Bed, It Can't Wait" + two paragraphs.
3. **Free for member organizations** — `.pb6-section`, "Free for Every Member Organization" + one paragraph.
4. **Impact** — `.pb6-section`, "What This Has Actually Done" + a five-item bullet list, a closing paragraph, and the two centered teal CTA buttons.
5. **Bottom navy band** — `.pb0-navy` again, carrying the TEDx sentence and a responsive 16:9 YouTube embed (`.pb6-video-wrap`).

All three body sections share the same 720px centered column, so their headings sit on one axis with the navy bands above and below.
