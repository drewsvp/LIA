## 1. Purpose

The public About page for the Love in Action Database. It opens with the "Join us in Making a Difference" navy band moved off PB-00, then explains how the program connects givers to real-time needs, describes what the database gives Alliance Member organizations, places it within the wider program alongside the drives and supply closet, and offers the two primary calls to action. It closes with a navy band carrying Heidi White's TEDx talk as an embedded video.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Arrives from:** the ABOUT link in the global navigation (MP-02), the About link in the public footer, and direct links.

**Leaves to:**
- `/items` — public item browse (Provide an Item button, client-side)
- `/volunteer` — public volunteer browse (Volunteer Your Time button, client-side)
- YouTube — the embedded TEDx video plays in place; the viewer may follow it out to youtube.com through the player's own controls.

## 3. Data

**Reads:** Nothing.
**Writes:** Nothing.
**Functions called:** None.
**Never touches:** anything. This surface is content only.

## 4. Layout regions

Top to bottom:

1. **Top navy band** — "Join us in Making a Difference" heading + the "Each of us have resources…" paragraph. Reuses `.pb0-navy` / `.pb0-navy-heading` / `.pb0-navy-para`. This is the page headline, not one section among several.
2. **Real-time needs** — `.pb6-section`, "Meet Real Needs in Real Time" + one paragraph.
3. **Value to member organizations** — `.pb6-section`, "A Powerful Tool for Local Organizations" + one paragraph.
4. **Wider program** — `.pb6-section`, "One Part of a Larger Program" + a three-item bullet list, two closing paragraphs, and the two centered teal CTA buttons.
5. **Bottom navy band** — `.pb0-navy` again, carrying the "Let's Change Our Community Together" heading, the TEDx sentence, and a responsive 16:9 YouTube embed (`.pb6-video-wrap`).

All three body sections share the same 720px centered column, so their headings sit on one axis with the navy bands above and below.
