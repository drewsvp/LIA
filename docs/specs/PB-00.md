## 1. Purpose

The public entry point to the whole system. Introduces the database, routes visitors to browsing items or volunteering, offers the weekly digest, and links out to member login.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Arrives from:** the site root URL, the main site navigation, and external links to the database home page.

**Leaves to:**
- PB-01 `/items`, via the Provide an Item tile.
- PB-03 `/volunteer`, via the Volunteer Your Time tile.
- PB-05 `/subscribe`, via the Weekly Email Sign Up button.
- MP-01 `/login`, via the Alliance Member Login link.

## 3. Data

**Reads:** Nothing.
**Writes:** Nothing.
**Functions called:** None.
**Never touches:** anything. This surface is navigation only.

## 4. Layout regions

Hero with site branding, "Alliance Member Login >" link, right-aligned below the hero, two paragraphs of introductory copy, two large image tiles ("Provide an Item," links to PB-01; "Volunteer Your Time," links to PB-03), then the digest callout with a "Weekly Email Sign Up" button linking to PB-05.

The navy "Join Us in Changing the World" / TEDx section captured on this page moved to PB-06 (About) and no longer renders here.
